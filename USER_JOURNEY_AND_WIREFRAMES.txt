1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================
Problem Statement:
Blinkit users display extreme frequency (3-5 orders/week) for high-frequency,
low-margin replenishment categories (milk, bread, onions, coriander). However,
they rarely cross over into high-margin lifestyle categories (Apparel/Innerwear,
Beauty & Skincare, WFH Electronics, OTC Wellness) due to:
  1. Fit & sizing anxiety for apparel.
  2. Perceived price inflation on quick commerce for non-grocery goods.
  3. Search-driven mental model (users search for "milk" and immediately check out).
  4. Decision fatigue when choosing standalone unfamiliar brand items.

Solution Hypothesis:
By introducing "Banger Combos" (curated routine bundles with transparent
mathematical proof of savings), risk-free doorstep size replacements, and an
ambient "New on Blinkit" exploration loop, users are guided from high-frequency
grocery replenishment into repeat discovery habits.
